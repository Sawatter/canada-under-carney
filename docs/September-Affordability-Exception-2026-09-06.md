## Evidence gap

The published D band requires relief below 20% of identified annual household cost increases. Neither the required household coverage ratio nor the band and minus has been re-established. The former household benefit average and cost figures lacked a reproducible denominator and matching period in the checked source and repository history. Those claims have been removed and their previous wording preserved in the correction record.

Opened in the September evidence review: the [PBO benefit costing](https://www.pbo-dpb.ca/en/publications/LEG-2526-010-S--canada-groceries-essentials-benefit--allocation-canadienne-epicerie-besoins-essentiels) supports program-wide costs and benefit-design percentages. It does not supply the required representative household relief-to-cost comparison. Operating benefits, example households and slower inflation do not establish that ratio.

The gap remains a grade-moving E4 exception under [Carry-Forward Rules](v2/verification/Carry-Forward-Rules.md#2-carry-forward-not-allowed), specifically CF-BLOCK-3. This decision does not label the missing comparison verified, resolve the underlying evidence gap, or treat the blocked claim as a scoring input. It permits temporary retention of the previously displayed result as an exception to ordinary cycle closure.

## Why retain the prior display

Assigning another grade without a supported comparison would invent a scoring conclusion. Removing the dimension from the scores would change the frozen model and weights. The cycle therefore keeps the prior display with a visible exception label and Low confidence, while leaving the thresholds, modifier effects, grade-point mappings, weighting and dimension model unchanged. The declining arrow is also the retained prior display, not a newly established worsening of the unresolved ratio.

The strongest alternative is to defer the dimension's new adjudication until the required measure is supported. That remains the correct description of its scoring status. The exception allows the rest of the September evidence review to close locally while making that limit visible. It does not authorize indefinite retention or imply that the overall scores have been independently established without this limitation.

## Expiry and next action

This exception expires at the October cycle on October 1, 2026. Its review record carries that expiry, so it cannot silently become a normal held review or extend into another cycle.

Before the next cycle can close, establish a sourced household relief numerator, cost-increase denominator, population and common time period, then apply the unchanged rule. Do not invent a denominator from program-wide costs, silently renew this exception, or change a frozen threshold or formula.
