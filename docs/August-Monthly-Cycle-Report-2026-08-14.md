## Duplicate Accounting

- The ledger counts citation surfaces, not only URLs. The same source can legitimately support a metric, trigger, promise, and source list, which is why 337 citation surfaces resolve to 186 unique cited URLs.
- PMO news is retained as separate defence and major-project event watches because the trigger questions differ.
- The MPO national list supplies the denominator; the 18 project pages supply project-level stage evidence. They are not interchangeable duplicates.
- Approval and preferred-PM polling remain separate constructs. Five Nanos preferred-PM waves do not enter the approval mean.
- Exact touched-source rows intentionally repeat some cadence URLs so the August inspection date is visible without implying that every cadence row was due.
